Privacy Policy

  1. Introduction

Ignitro Innovative Solutions LLC (“Ignitro,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a digital marketing and advertising agency. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website or use our services.

By using our website or submitting information through our forms, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

 

  1. Information We Collect

When you fill out a contact form on our website, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number

We do NOT collect any payment or financial information.

We also automatically collect certain data when you visit our website, including:

  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time on site
  • Referring website or traffic source
  • IP address and general location
  • Device type and operating system

 

  1. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and communicate about our services
  • Understand how visitors use our website and improve user experience
  • Measure the performance of our advertising campaigns
  • Serve relevant ads on platforms such as Meta (Facebook/Instagram) and Google
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

 

  1. Third-Party Tools & Advertising Technologies

Meta Pixel (Facebook / Instagram): We use the Meta Pixel to measure ad effectiveness, optimize campaigns, and build audiences. The Meta Pixel may collect your IP address, browser info, and actions taken on our site. You can opt out at: facebook.com/privacy/explanation

Google Analytics: We use Google Analytics to understand how users interact with our website. Data collected is aggregated and anonymized. You can opt out at: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout

Cookies: Our website uses cookies to support browsing and advertising. Types include:

  • Essential cookies – required for the website to function
  • Analytics cookies – help us understand how visitors use our site
  • Advertising cookies – used to deliver relevant ads and measure performance

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling some cookies may affect website functionality.

 

  1. Sharing of Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it only in these cases:

  • Service Providers: Trusted vendors (hosting, analytics, ad platforms) who support our operations
  • Advertising Platforms: Meta and Google, to serve ads and measure performance
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights or safety
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

 

  1. Data Retention

We keep personal information collected through contact forms for as long as needed to fulfill the purposes in this policy or as required by law. To request deletion, contact us using the details in Section 10.

 

  1. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. However, no internet transmission or storage system is 100% secure.

 

  1. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal information we hold
  • Correction: Request we fix inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your information
  • Opt-Out: Opt out of your data being used for targeted advertising
  • Restriction: Request that we limit how we use your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us as described in Section 10.

 

  1. Children’s Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If we discover we have done so, we will delete it promptly.

 

  1. Contact Us

For any questions or requests related to this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

Ignitro Innovative Solutions LLC

Email: info@getignitro.com

Website: www.getignitro.com

 

  1. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the Effective Date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
