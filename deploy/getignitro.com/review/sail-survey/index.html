Oqood

Sell off-plan before the building exists.

What the law says

Off-plan sales made before project registration with DLD are void (Law 13/2008 as amended by 19/2017, Art. 3; Executive Council Resolution 6/2010 Art. 11).

What Sail Survey delivers

Unit area survey from the approved drawings, DLD-format unit data, unit plans — submission-ready.

02

Final registration

Hand over title deeds at completion.

What the law says

JOP plans and declarations are due within 60 days of the completion certificate (+30 extension), or DLD appoints another party at the developer’s cost. A net area more than 5% below the area sold triggers compensation.

What Sail Survey delivers

Completion survey, final sellable and common areas, JOP plans, Oqood-to-title-deed conversion.
