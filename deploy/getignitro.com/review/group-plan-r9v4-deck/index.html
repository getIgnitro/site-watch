How big is the market for everything Sail sells?

The market is the public count of launches, units and completions times Sail's own price list; where Sail has no rate the market rate is used. The 76‐project list is Sail's full record: a service not on it is a 0% share.

How does the market itself join my three companies?

The same developer who registers a launch with Sail needs a website and a launch event, reports on escrow during construction, needs a final survey at completion and an Ejari survey to lease. Every company, new or running, a developer or a Sky tenant, needs a licence, an office, an Ejari, visas, books and renewals.

The market runs as two chains, the developer's project and the company, new or running, and all 27 of the group's services sit on their links.
