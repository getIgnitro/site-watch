SailNova Group

How big is the market for everything Sail sells?

The market is the public count of launches, units and completions times Sail's own price list; where Sail has no rate the market rate is used. The 76‐project list is Sail's full record: a service not on it is a 0% share.

BIM, mandatory above G+12 or 15,000 m2 from 1 Apr 2026, is priced once on Sail's own 76 projects and left out of the total.

Sail sells one of its services and holds about 8% of it; every other service it lists has a market and no Sail share.

What can each brand take a month?

The drawing step is the gate: every Oqood set passes through the same 7 people, so today the team, not the market, sets Sail’s ceiling.

The final survey still needs site measurement, and BIM work needs 3D modelling staff the drawing team does not have today.

Sail’s team draws ~18‐21 towers a month and delivers 6.7; with the app, each set submitted within 3 hours, it can take every launch and completion in Dubai.
