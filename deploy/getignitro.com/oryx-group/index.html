02 · What we would do

Three brands, three different jobs.

One team, one calendar, one invoice — but the brands are not in the same place, so they do not get the same plan.

Nando’s Qatar

Put all 12 branches on camera.

One shoot day a month that rotates branch to branch, so every restaurant gets its turn. The flame grill, the PERi-PERi ritual, the people behind the counter. Arabic and English on every caption, all 12 Google profiles current, every review answered.

Ocean Basket Qatar

Make one restaurant a destination.

With a single location the content has to be worth a drive. One film a month built around one dish, platters and family tables for the weekend, and paid reach aimed at the Mall of Qatar catchment rather than all of Doha.

Oodles Wok Qatar

Get the brand known, fast.

Under a thousand followers with two counters open is the clearest gap in the group. Loud, quick wok content, price-led stories and ads pointed at the office-lunch and student crowd in both malls. The job is recognition, not polish.
