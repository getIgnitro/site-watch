The fine print

$0 setup, month to month, cancel anytime, files are yours.

Filming is not included; a local videographer day can be added at cost.

How a month works

  1. 1

    Onboarding, at $0.

    Your brand kit, a profile tune-up and your first 30-day calendar. You connect your accounts through a link we send.

  2. 2

    A 2-minute shot list.

    Each month you get a short list of clips to film on your phone, and a link to upload them. No clips that month? We make text-led videos instead.

  3. 3

    Review the month by link.

    Every post comes to you for review before it goes out, with one round of changes.

  4. 4

    We post and report.

    Posts go out on schedule in your US time zone, and a report follows every month.

If we do not hear back within 5 days of sending your month for review, it goes live as sent. You can edit or pull any post before it publishes.
