How much does a website cost per month?

By the KN Services team · 31 October 2026 · 5 minute read

The short answer: a small business website costs about $4 to $1,000 a month, depending on who runs it.

  • A website builder such as Wix or Squarespace: about $12 to $40 a month on the standard plans. Store plans cost more.
  • Your own site on your own hosting: roughly $4 to $15 a month for hosting and a domain, before anyone maintains it.
  • Maintenance: about $50 to $150 a month from a freelancer, $100 to $1,000 from an agency.
  • An all-in monthly plan (design, hosting and upkeep in one fee): ours is $99 to $499 a month, $0 upfront.

Below: where those numbers come from, what a monthly fee covers, and the extras added later.

What a monthly fee usually includes

A fair monthly fee covers what keeps the site online and safe:

  • Hosting: the server space your site lives on.
  • The domain: your web address. Builders often include it free for the first year only, then bill it separately.
  • An SSL certificate: the padlock in the browser bar. Many hosts now include a free one.
  • Backups: a copy of your site you can restore if something breaks.
  • Updates: keeping the software current and secure.
  • Support: someone to call when something breaks.

On a builder, the last four are mostly your job.

What it usually does not include

New pages, a redesign, writing, photos, ads and social media are normally extra, as are changes beyond the monthly allowance.

The hidden monthly extras

The headline price is rarely the whole bill. Watch for these:

  • Premium plugins and apps. Forms, reviews and other add-ons often carry their own fee.
  • Business email. A mailbox at your own domain is usually extra. Wix sells Google Workspace email separately; Squarespace includes one account free for the first year on Core and Advanced only.
  • Booking tools. Appointment booking often needs a higher plan or a paid add-on.
  • Transaction fees on store builders. Squarespace takes 2% of store sales on Basic, 0% on Core and Advanced. Shopify takes 2%, 1% or 0.6% by plan if you use another payment provider. Card fees come on top.
  • Renewals. Domains bought through a builder renew at about $15 to $30 a year, and builder plans can renew at $18 to $60 a month once any introductory price ends.

How to compare two monthly quotes

Two quotes of $99 a month can be very different deals. Check:

  1. The total for 12 and 36 months, including any setup fee.
  2. What each includes, line by line: hosting, domain, SSL, backups, updates, support, email.
  3. How many changes a month are included.
  4. Whether the price is a yearly plan paid upfront, and what it renews at.
  5. Whether the domain is registered in your name.
  6. What happens when you cancel: do you keep the site and domain?

The last matters most. Some monthly plans are rent: stop paying and the site is gone. Others end with you owning the site. Weighing a monthly fee against one payment?
