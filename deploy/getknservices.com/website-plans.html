The fine print

Pay once: one payment, and the site and domain are yours from day one. The first year of hosting, domain, mailbox and small updates is included; from year two keep us running it from $39 a month, or take your files.

Pay monthly: $0 setup, cancel anytime. Stay 12 paid months and the site and domain are yours; cancel earlier and the site comes down.

Your customers pay you directly. Invoices and store orders are paid into your own Stripe account, in your business name, and paid out to your bank. Stripe's fees are yours: currently 0.4% per paid invoice, plus Stripe's card processing fees. We never hold your money.

How you pay us: once by card or bank transfer, or monthly by bank debit or card. No setup fee either way.

Search: we do the work and send the report. Nobody can promise a position on Google, and we do not.
